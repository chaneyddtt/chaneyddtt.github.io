Before that, I did my PhD at the same department supervised by Prof.Gim Hee Lee, and Master in the Electronic Engneering Department at Beihang University, supervised by Prof. Xia Mao.

Research Field

My research focuses on 3D Computer Vision and Deep Learning. Specifically, I have been working on 3D human/animal pose modeling and motion reasoning, 3D general scene modeling and understanding, human-scene interaction modeling.
